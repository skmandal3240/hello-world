# Top 10 App Ideas Mass Users Want — Rated

A curated list of high-demand app ideas evaluated across four dimensions to help prioritize what to build next.

---

## Rating Methodology

Each idea is scored **out of 10** across four dimensions:

| Dimension | What it measures |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Demand** | How large and urgent is the user need? |
| **Feasibility** | How realistic is it to build with a small team? |
| **Monetization** | How strong is the revenue potential? |
| **Uniqueness** | How differentiated from existing solutions? |

**Overall** = average of the four scores.

## The Rankings
